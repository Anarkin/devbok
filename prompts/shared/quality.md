## Quality bar

- Let the topic decide the shape and size: derive the structure from the topic itself, never pad to reach a number, never merge unrelated things to stay under one, and say so plainly when the topic genuinely can't sustain a target.
- Modern-first, legacy-aware: show the currently recommended approach, but explicitly name the older pattern I will meet in existing codebases — interviews reward navigating both.
- Be precise about versions: label features with the version where they became stable, default, or deprecated. Never present a deprecated pattern as current.
- Specific over vague: concrete numbers, symptoms, and decisions make things memorable — no hand-waving, no filler.
- Link the relevant official documentation page per unit so I can go deeper.
- No assumptions about me beyond the topic text: not my employer, industry, codebase, or the rest of my stack.
